# Operations activation validation

## Activated capabilities

- Shared Hindsight-first memory, task and operations procedures are installed and published.
- Default Hermes fresh-provider routing resolves to one explicit business bank across tested platform/user identities. A real user business decision was captured by the installed sync-turn hook and exact-read, then recalled by fresh providers. Existing running UI/gateway providers were not restarted; this is not proof every existing session reloaded.
- A real bounded integrated review used incoming/sent mail, complete selected threads and an attachment, Surface project/task/order inventories, and JobTread jobs/tasks/to-dos. Findings, pending coverage, associations and a derived report were retained and exact-read from company memory. A repeated same-scope pass carried proposal identities without additions.
- Review performed zero business mutations because notification and stronger cross-tool serialization prerequisites were not established. This proves integrated review/continuity, not write-path creation deduplication or crash-safe external execution.
- Local online Hindsight backup is scheduled daily. An actual logical snapshot was restored into independent disposable PostgreSQL, with row counts and row-hash multisets compared for all archived tables. Production service remained running. A fresh backup was triggered after the review completed.
- The existing morning email job was updated in place to the company operations briefing at the existing local morning schedule, preserving its owner delivery destination and continuity. Configuration was read back. A bounded actual scheduler run was dispatched; its execution/delivery outcome must be checked separately before claiming it passed.

## Current limits

- Initial company baseline is explicitly incomplete; pending candidate threads and legacy backlog are preserved. Run success does not imply complete historical coverage.
- No automatic source sync, multi-writer guarantees, external-action exactly-once claim or business-write crash recovery is established.
- Local backups do not protect against loss of the machine. An encrypted off-machine destination has not been authorized. Database restoration was tested; full application failover with recovered credentials/worker state was not.
- No historical memory-bank merge/import, production service restart or unrelated profile change occurred.
- Exact private evidence and customer reports live outside this public repository. Schedule/mailbox/bank/account identifiers are runtime settings, not reusable policy.

## Checks

Repository unit/structural checks, synthetic Hindsight exact-document tests, real integrated read/repeat tests and isolated backup restore each establish different evidence. Do not substitute one for another. Keep pending scheduler outcome and remaining limits visible in rollout reporting.
